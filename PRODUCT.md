# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS — approved by the user; no build step, deployable to GitHub Pages

## Users

Recruiters, collaborators, and people who meet AR Saadhana online and want to
reach their profiles or email. They usually arrive from a shared link, often on
a phone, and want one tap to the right place.

## Product Purpose

A single public page that gathers AR Saadhana's profiles — GitHub, LinkedIn,
Instagram, Twitter/X, email — in one place. Success means a visitor finds the
right link in under a minute and the page feels deliberate and professional.

## Positioning

A personal link hub without platform branding. It presents education (IIT
Madras, then CUTN) and the person's own profiles, not a third-party service.

## Operating Context

Shared from resumes, messages, and social bios. Opened on mobile and desktop.
No login, no backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five outbound links: GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, email.
- Education block: IIT Madras first, then CUTN.
- Static page; no JavaScript, no analytics, no third-party runtime requests.
  Gloock and Manrope are self-hosted.
- Monogram placeholder until a headshot is supplied.
- AmbitionBox omitted because the profile is private; the second email omitted.
- Degree/program and years were not supplied.

## Brand Commitments

Name: AR Saadhana. No tagline in v1.

## Evidence on Hand

Real profile URLs supplied by the user (see
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-portfolio-design.md`). No headshot and no
degree details; neither may be invented.

## Product Principles

- One tap to every profile.
- Professional without pretending: no invented credentials or photos.
- Fast and quiet: no scripts, no tracking, no external assets.
- Mobile-first; legible and keyboard-accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px tap targets, and
`prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
